IMPLEMENTATION NOTE:
This document is an enterprise compliance framework for a global AI - assisted fintech, payments, remittance, banking, credit, lending, investment and blockchain corporation.It must be mapped to Nordi Remitwave's actual legal entities, licences, products, jurisdictions, Partners and risk appetite before use. It does not create a licence or regulatory permission.

1. PURPOSE
The purpose of this Compliance Framework is to establish Nordi Remitwave's minimum enterprise standards for:

legal and regulatory compliance;
financial - crime prevention;
prudential and conduct risk;
customer protection;
payments;
lending;
investments;
Digital Assets;
AI governance;
data protection;
cybersecurity;
operational resilience;
complaints;
third - party risk;
regulatory reporting;
audits;
investigations;
employee conduct; and
board oversight.
The framework is designed to support lawful operation across multiple jurisdictions while allowing local entities to maintain stricter procedures where required.

2. COMPLIANCE PHILOSOPHY
Nordi Remitwave shall not launch a regulated product based solely on technical readiness.

A product may launch only after:

legal perimeter analysis;

financial - crime review;
cybersecurity review;
operational - resilience review;
customer - disclosure review;

confirmation that required controls are operational.

Digital Asset compliance;

3.4 Three lines model
First line: Business, product, operations and engineering own day - to - day risks and controls.

Second line: Compliance and Risk provide independent oversight, challenge, policy and monitoring.

Third line: Internal Audit provides independent assurance.
